KommuAssist 2 is Level 2 driver assistance — you stay in control, but the system shares the workload on routes you drive every week.

What it helps with

  • Adaptive cruise control — maintains speed and following distance on highways and fast roads
  • Lane keeping assist — helps keep you centred with gentle steering support
  • Traffic-aware behaviour — smoother responses in stop-and-go congestion
  • Road understanding — tuned for curves, traffic lights, and speed bumps common on Malaysian roads
  • Driver monitoring — ensures you remain attentive while assist is active

Under the hood runs bukapilot v10 — an end-to-end neural driving stack with lateral and longitudinal control, built on open-source roots and improved by a global community with 100M+ miles of real-world driving data.

Everything processes on-device in your car. No cloud dependency while you're driving.
